# Master prompt requirements, sections 3-15 (faithful requirement extraction by parent agent from the user's full prompt; every bullet preserved in condensed form)

## 3 Non-negotiable constraints
3.1 No AI/LLM/chatbot at runtime: no AI emotional interpretation, personality profiling, journal analysis, answer grading, recommendations from personal entries, conversational coaching, inference about psychological condition. Use pre-authored content, deterministic rules, local assessment, transparent scoring.
3.2 No runtime external APIs, cloud DB, accounts/auth, remote servers, third-party analytics, ads/tracking SDKs, remote AI/recommendations, remote CDN for ordinary learning. All lessons, scenarios, assessment logic, feedback, scoring, assets packaged. Static hosting OK; external research links only on deliberate user action.
3.3 Local-first: store completed lessons, attempts & scores, mastery, review schedules, scenario responses/decision histories, journal, preferences, baseline/follow-up results locally; never transmit. Controls: export (documented portable format), import backup, delete individual journal entries, reset progress, delete all data (explicit, explained). Validate imports; handle malformed files, incompatible versions, interrupted imports safely; never silently overwrite existing data.
3.4 Privacy transparency: IndexedDB not encrypted, never claim encryption; explain browser storage limits (clearing data, private browsing, device access risk). Store no secrets.
3.5 Offline after SW cache: curriculum nav, lessons, exercises, assessment & scoring, scenarios, progress, reflection, bundled research library, export/import, settings & deletion. Clearly distinguish first visit (download needed) from later offline use.
3.6 Future Android (Capacitor): storage behind interface so native storage can replace IndexedDB without rewriting curriculum/engine. Do not build Android now.

## 4 Research integrity
4.1 Research register before final curriculum; primary research, systematic reviews, books, institutional sources; verify suggested refs (Gross 1998 doi 10.1037/1089-2680.2.3.271; Gross 2015 doi 10.1080/1047840X.2014.940781; Beck CBT Basics and Beyond, verify edition; WHO 2021 Doing What Matters in Times of Stress, note context limits; Dunlosky et al 2013 doi 10.1111/j.1539-6053.2013.01003.x; Eisenstadt et al 2021 JMIR Mental Health 8(11) e31170 doi 10.2196/31170; Macrynikola et al 2024 npj Mental Health Research 3:14 doi 10.1038/s44184-023-00048-5). Unverifiable refs must not be presented as verified.
4.2 Source-to-claim register: per source stable ID, exact title/authors/year/publication/DOI or URL, source type, population/setting, design & sample size, outcomes, relevant findings, limitations, which lesson/technique/claim uses it, whether direct claim / cautious extrapolation / background. Distinguish: (1) process/technique may be useful, (2) digital delivery can support outcome, (3) specific intervention evaluated, (4) this app improves outcomes - not equivalent. Clinical-population and mindfulness-app evidence do not validate this app.
4.3 Keep limitations visible (2021 review: few studies for ER outcome, high heterogeneity, methodological limits; 2024 review: mixed process findings, engagement/method limits). Never invent effect sizes, results, citations, DOIs, validation status, clinical recommendations, consensus, causation from correlation. Say when evidence is mixed/limited/indirect/absent.
4.4 No false precision: no "emotionally intelligent" from one score; no questionnaire measures all competence; no prevention claims; no technique works for everyone; mastery threshold not a validated cutoff; high score != healthy; low score != diagnosis/trait. Thresholds labelled design choices.
4.5 IP: no copyrighted questionnaires, proprietary assessments, paid course material, long book passages. For any standardised measure verify purpose, validity/reliability for population, nonclinical self-guided suitability, licensing, scoring/interpretation restrictions; else use original labelled educational reflection; never present custom questionnaire as validated.

## 5 Curriculum
5.1 Complete 12-week programme, ~60 substantive sessions (~5/week); allow slower 12-16+ weeks without penalties; sessions ~15-30 min plus optional short daily practice; state realistic durations from actual content. Not "complete" until full curriculum, practice, assessments, feedback, research mapping present. No padding by splitting paragraphs or duplicating exercises.
5.2 Phases (minimum, refine by evidence):
P1 Baseline/orientation/foundational model: goals & baseline; emotion vs thought vs interpretation vs impulse vs behaviour; training method & how mastery is shown; simple episode observation without overanalysis; limits of self-report & purpose of reassessment.
P2 Awareness/differentiation: naming emotions; closely related emotions; bodily signals & action tendencies; primary reactions vs later interpretations; intensity/duration/context/change; uncertainty in own interpretations.
P3 Appraisal/cognition: facts vs interpretations; assumptions & predictions; habitual thinking patterns without treating every negative thought as distorted; alternative explanations; evidence & uncertainty; rumination/repetitive negative thinking; when reflection is useful vs unproductive.
P4 In-the-moment regulation: pause between impulse & action; flexible attention; breathing/grounding/simple physiological practices where evidence & safety support; reappraisal; problem-solving vs acceptance; changing situation vs changing response; strategy by context not one universal technique; when emotional expression is appropriate.
P5 Anger/criticism/provocation: irritation & frustration; criticism; perceived disrespect; escalation patterns; respond/delay/disengage/set boundary; assertive vs aggressive vs passive; de-escalation without surrendering legitimate interests.
P6 Anxiety/uncertainty/disappointment: uncertainty without demanding certainty; anticipated negative outcomes; preparation vs worry; setbacks & unmet expectations; controllable vs uncontrollable; acting while uncomfortable; protective vs unnecessarily restrictive avoidance.
P7 Professional composure/interpersonal: difficult feedback; disagreeing with colleague or authority; unreasonable requests & conflicting priorities; boundaries; decisions under pressure; conflict with unequal power/incentives; responding to bad behaviour without automatic escalation; not treating every disagreement as personal threat.
P8 Recurring patterns/behavioural review: review repeated episodes; recurring triggers & response sequences; observed patterns vs causal hypotheses; reinforcement & short-term relief; consequences of habitual responses; small observable behavioural experiments; revising hypotheses when unsupported.
P9 Integration/transfer/maintenance: multiple skills in complex situations; unfamiliar scenarios; context-dependent choices; cumulative retrieval; reviewing past mistakes; sustainable maintenance plan; post-programme assessment; optional follow-up scheduling.
5.3 Every session: title & purpose; specific objectives; concise accurate explanation; concrete example; practical exercise; >=1 interactive activity; feedback explaining why; explanation-back where appropriate; mastery assessment or specified dependency; actionable summary; practical application task; research source links; estimated duration; prerequisites & progression rules; remediation for strugglers. Vary formats. No vague "stay calm/be mindful/think positively": say what to notice, what action, when appropriate, limits/trade-offs.

## 6 Three-layer system
L1 interactive (MCQ, predict consequence, sequence, match, classify fact/interpretation/emotion/impulse/action, next step, compare responses, strategy for context, short branching sims); not passive only; feedback explains principle and why plausible alternatives less suitable in context.
L2 explanation-back: own words/apply/describe use; free text; never pretend to grade; transparent rubric, model answer/key points, compare against rubric, self-assessment, optional revise, clear notice app did not evaluate prose. Example rubric: identifies concept, explains mechanism, recognises context, proposes relevant action. Self-rating is reflective, not verified competence.
L3 mastery: objective/rule-scored; knowledge, scenario decisions, new examples, cumulative earlier items, delayed retrieval, alternative items for same skill; never same question as sole evidence. Per skill define competency, evidence, scoring rules, threshold (80% allowed, labelled design choice), remediation path, reassessment conditions.
6.1 Completion, mastery, retention separate in data model and UI. Completion != mastery. Failed mastery -> targeted remediation + different assessment; keep reasonable history; no punishment. No overall EI score combining unrelated measures.
6.2 Spaced review tied to learning objectives (short, longer, later cumulative scenario); schedule configurable instructional choice, not universal rule. Prioritise retrieval/application/correction over rereading.

## 7 Emotion Regulation Gym
>=36 fully authored scenarios; realistic situation, plausible alternatives, consequences, educational feedback.
7.1 >=6 each: (1) anger/irritation/provocation (2) criticism/perceived disrespect/difficult feedback (3) anxiety/uncertainty/anticipated failure (4) frustration/disappointment/setbacks (5) interpersonal misunderstanding/relationship friction (6) professional conflict/decisions/boundaries/competing demands. Coverage genuine, not relabelled.
7.2 Each: concise narrative with enough context; relevant facts & useful ambiguity; challenge; decision point; >=3 plausible options; consequence/pathway per option; specific feedback on strengths & limits; follow-up decision when useful; reflection/transfer question; principle explanation; skill tag & sources. Not one obviously virtuous answer vs absurd ones; legitimate trade-offs (delay useful vs evasive; assertiveness despite discomfort). Feedback reflects context, safety, competing objectives, uncertainty.
7.3 Branching where appropriate; plausible proportionate consequences; never imply one perfect response controls others; options may yield trade-offs not one superior result.
7.4 Track by skill & scenario type, not single score; new/alternative scenarios for reassessment; revisit difficult scenarios without shame/punitive language.

## 8 Baseline / post / follow-up
8.1 Baseline: goals; self-reported confidence in selected skills; short objective knowledge set; small scenario application set; optional reflection on recurring situations; explain it is an educational starting point, not diagnosis.
8.2 Post: knowledge; scenario performance; ability to explain selected concepts; self-reported confidence/perceived change; retention of earlier skills; use alternative/unseen items.
8.3 Optional follow-ups ~2 and ~6 weeks after; postpone/disable; results distinguish knowledge retention, scenario performance, self-report; no merged improvement claim.
8.4 Investigate ERQ (reappraisal & suppression habits; not all competence) and DERS-16 (self-reported difficulties; not performance test): verify permissions, administration, scoring, relevance; else omit and use original labelled nonvalidated measures. Never diagnose or label deficient.

## 9 Screens
9.1 Onboarding: purpose; can/cannot do; pathway & realistic time; privacy/local storage disclosure; training vs assessment vs clinical care; short goal setting; baseline; concise; revisitable later.
9.2 Dashboard: current phase; next recommended lesson; current learning priorities; lessons completed; skills at mastery threshold; skills due for review; practical next action; no vanity metrics, streaks, guilt warnings, endless notifications.
9.3 Curriculum browser: phases, sessions, prerequisites, completion & mastery status; what's next and why; revisit completed lessons without disrupting sequence.
9.4 Lesson reader: readable content, examples, activities, sources, clear progression; maintain progress safely across close/reload.
9.5 Assessment centre: lesson assessments, explanation-back, remediation, cumulative, delayed retrieval, post-programme, follow-up; transparent scoring & completion requirements.
9.6 Gym: discovery & filter by skill, situation, difficulty; practise, review feedback, retry alternative scenarios.
9.7 Journal: optional private local; fields: what happened / noticed / interpretation / emotion or impulse / what I did / what happened next / try next time; all optional; edit & delete; no forced daily journalling; no auto interpretation/hidden causes/traits.
9.8 Progress & insights: by skill, assessment, review history; self-report vs demonstrated performance separated; no clinical labels, user comparisons, invented norms.
9.9 Research library: verified citations, DOI/links, descriptions, lessons & claims supported, limitations; mark direct evidence vs background theory vs cautious extrapolation.
9.10 Settings: export, import, reset, delete all, review & journal preferences, reminder controls if reminders exist, privacy info, about/evidence limits/version. All destructive ops need clear confirmation.

## 10 Architecture
10.1 React, strict TS, Vite, lightweight accessible styling, IndexedDB, service worker, manifest, Vitest, Playwright, GitHub Actions. Justify substitutions. Avoid unnecessary deps/heavy state mgmt/backends/low-value UI libs.
10.2 Content as typed structured data: stable IDs, objectives, skill tags, prerequisites, sections, activities, answer keys, feedback, rubrics, mastery criteria, remediation links, source refs, versioning. Build-time validation: missing feedback, invalid refs, duplicate IDs, broken curriculum links fail tests/build.
10.3 Storage interface with only needed ops; IndexedDB impl with explicit schema versions & safe migrations; UI independent of IndexedDB details; Android adapter can implement same interface.
10.4 Typed versioned structures: preferences, lesson progress, attempts, mastery, scenario history, review scheduling, journal, baseline/follow-up, export/import envelopes. Stable IDs & timestamps. Prevent duplicate progress records, partial imports, invalid score ranges, silent data loss. Testable migrations; recovery for incompatible/corrupt local data.
10.5 Versioned caching of shell & assets. Test: first load, offline reload, offline lesson completion, offline assessment, offline persistence, app updates, cache replacement, recovery from failed updates, compatibility between app versions & stored data. Never cache broken/partial release indefinitely. Explain how users get updates & how data preserved.
10.6 Audit built app for network activity: no analytics, fonts, APIs, CDNs, remote DBs, external scripts, trackers. Bundle fonts/assets. Document unavoidable hosting/browser dependencies.
10.7 GitHub Pages via repeatable Actions workflow: correct Vite base; assets resolve; permissions & triggers; build before deploy; test production routing & asset paths; SW scope & manifest paths on deployed site; document repo & URL patterns. Verify; never claim deployed because config exists.
10.8 Installable PWA; document Capacitor path (storage compatibility, Android testing, permissions, release); no native deps now.

## 11 UX
11.1 Calm restrained hierarchy, readability, clear nav, low cognitive load, consistent spacing/type, meaningful progress, accessible contrast, distinct visual treatment for explanation / practice / feedback / assessment, mobile-first and good on desktop, fast, minimal friction. Colour purposeful, never sole signal. Avoid gradients, decorative charts, excess cards, meaningless stats, distracting animation.
11.2 WCAG 2.2 AA: semantic HTML, keyboard nav, visible focus, screen-reader-friendly controls & status messages, labels & instructions, contrast, reduced motion, accessible validation/errors, touch targets, alternatives to drag-only/colour-only. Test real interactions.
11.3 Test narrow mobile, tablet, desktop; no horizontal overflow, clipping, tiny controls, dense assessment screens; scenario choices, explanation fields, feedback, charts usable on phone.
11.4 Direct respectful language; no infantilising; ordinary reactions not failures; no shame copy, exaggerated claims, false urgency, manipulative engagement.

## 12 Safety
Educational self-training tool; not medical device, therapist, crisis service, or substitute for care; proportionate visible boundaries. Do not: diagnose; claim to treat; promise prevention; encourage enduring abuse/harassment/unsafe situations for the sake of regulation; present compliance or silence as ideal response to mistreatment; treat all anger as irrational or all anxiety as harmful; tell users they are responsible for others' behaviour; interpret journal as proof of trauma/personality disorder/hidden cause. Threat/coercion/harassment/danger scenarios prioritise safety, boundaries, appropriate support, context. Teach regulating own response != tolerating unacceptable behaviour. Calm non-alarmist safety message; avoid unverified crisis resources/clinical claims for user's jurisdiction.

## 13 QA automated checks
13.1 Curriculum: full 12 weeks; ~60 substantive sessions; valid objectives & content; required exercises & assessments; valid source refs where claims need evidence; prerequisites valid IDs; remediation links resolve; six categories meet minimum; >=36 scenarios; all options have feedback; scoring complete & deterministic; mastery criteria explicit; no placeholder presented as finished.
13.2 Assessment: completion vs mastery separate; free text never shown as AI-graded; mastery follows documented rules; alternative items for important skills; wrong answers trigger useful remediation; repeated attempts don't corrupt records; delayed review based on objectives; cumulative includes earlier skills; scenario feedback context-sensitive; thresholds not described as clinically validated.
13.3 Research: citations real & accurate; bibliographic details match; claims map to sources; limitations documented; direct vs extrapolation distinguished; copyright/licensing respected; unverified sources identified; no unsupported efficacy claims in marketing/onboarding/UI.
13.4 Functional tests: navigation & routing; every major screen; lesson progression; scoring; mastery & remediation; scenario branching; persistence; journal create/edit/delete; export/import; full reset; schema migrations; offline; keyboard accessibility; mobile responsiveness; production build; GitHub Pages paths; SW updates; runtime network requests. Dev server launch is not evidence.
