# Curriculum and assessment design

## Structure
12 weeks, 9 phases, 60 sessions (36 lessons, 12 practice, 3 reviews, 9 checkpoints). Sessions unlock in order; each lists its prerequisite.

## Session anatomy (spec 5.3)
1. Teaching (>= 150 words in lessons and practice), evidence links.
2. Worked example.
3. **Layer 1 activity**: classify (18), sequence (9), match (9), compare (8), predict (4). Every item or option has its own feedback. Controls are native radios, selects and move-up/move-down buttons, so no drag is needed. Activities are practice and do not count towards mastery.
4. Practice exercise (real-world steps).
5. **Layer 2 explanation-back**: prompt, visible five-criterion rubric (accuracy, own words, example, boundaries, use), model key points, self-rating, revise option. The app states that it does not grade prose. Checkpoints and reviews use a phase-level prompt built from the phase's key points.
6. **Layer 3 knowledge check**: original items plus one alternative per lesson. Retakes show least-seen items first.
7. Summary (key points), application task, sources, optional reflection, remediation links.

## Duration estimate
`minutes = words/180 + practice steps x 3 + activity items x 0.75 + 1 + 6 (explain-back) + questions x 1 (x 1.25 at checkpoints) + 2 (application plan)`. Range 18 to 26, mean 21.6 minutes. This is an estimate, not a measurement.

## Mastery and retention
- **Phase mastery**: checkpoint >= 80% (design choice). Each attempt draws 8 items from the phase pool (original + alternative items), least exposed first, plus up to 2 cumulative items from earlier phases.
- **Skill mastery** (per lesson): latest checkpoint/review answers on >= 2 of the skill's items, >= 80% correct. Lesson-time answers do not count, so skill mastery needs delayed evidence.
- **Remediation**: missed items link to their lessons.
- **Retention**: after phase mastery, items return at 2, 7 and 21 days; a miss resets to 2 days. The review screen lists the objectives under review.

## Scenario gym
40 scenarios in the six spec 7.1 categories, each with difficulty 1 to 3, skill tag and context. 8 have branching follow-ups (one per first choice) with trade-off feedback. Every scenario ends with a category transfer question that can be saved to the journal.

## Baseline, post, follow-ups
Baseline: goals, 8 self-ratings, confidence 0 to 10, 6 knowledge items (separate from lesson items), 8 situational items. Post adds an ungraded explanation. Follow-ups at about 14 and 42 days after the post check, shown on Today, postponable by a week, can be turned off. Each result is stored separately.
